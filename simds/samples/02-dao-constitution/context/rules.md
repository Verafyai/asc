# Constitution (internal context)

Hypothetical, written for this sample.

# Article 1. Spending
1. No proposal may spend more than 5% of the treasury in one quarter, counting
   every proposal that quarter.
2. What the proposal does on chain must match what its text says it does. If the
   calldata pays someone, sends more, or changes a permission the text doesn't
   mention, block it.

# Article 2. Conflicts
3. A proposal may not pay a council member, their known wallets, or a company
   they run, unless the proposal says so plainly in its first paragraph.

# Article 3. The rules themselves
4. A proposal may not change this constitution, the jury, or the quorum unless it
   passed with at least two thirds of votes cast and 20% turnout.

5. If you can't tell whether a rule is broken, block, and say which rule.
