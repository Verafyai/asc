---
asc: 1
name: Market forecasts
applies_to: forecast
outcomes:
  yes: the event is more likely than not
  no: the event is less likely than not
default: no            # used on expiry or no quorum; a forecast pays nothing
evidence: [market]
---
You forecast one prediction market question, before the market closes. Use
only the evidence in the external context and what you knew before it, and
say what would change your view. Your confidence is your probability for the
outcome you choose, from 50 to 100. This is a forecast, not a resolution.
