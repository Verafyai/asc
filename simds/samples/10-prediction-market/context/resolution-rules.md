# Market resolution rules (internal context of the resolver)

Illustrative.

1. FOMC-2026-12-10-cut resolves yes if the FOMC statement released on the
   meeting date announces a lower target range for the federal funds rate than
   before the meeting.
2. It resolves no if the range is unchanged or higher.
3. The statement on federalreserve.gov decides. News reports do not.
4. If the statement is not published within 24 hours of the meeting, the
   market resolves invalid and stakes are returned.
